AI Meeting Notes for Finance

Built to serve regulated financial institutions.

Built to serve regulated financial institutions.

Fellow is the secure AI meeting notetaker built for private equity, hedge funds, investment banks, asset managers, fintechs, RIAs, and broker-dealers: teams where meetings are subject to SEC recordkeeping, FINRA supervision, and internal compliance review.

Compliance-first by design

Compliance-first by design

SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR, and CCPA ready with MNPI-aware workflows, BAA available, and a strict no-training policy: Fellow never uses your meeting data to train AI models.

Record with consent, share with control

Record with consent, share with control

AI meeting recordings and summaries from Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and in-person conversations are securely stored in Fellow with access controls that help keep confidential content limited to the right people. Admins can manage recording policies across the organization, while consent capture helps notify participants and document consent before recording begins. Password-protected recaps provide an extra layer of access control for especially sensitive conversations.

Zero-day retention, MNPI redaction, full audit trail

Zero-day retention, MNPI redaction, full audit trail

Retention can be configured independently for recordings/transcripts and AI summaries. MNPI can be redacted from transcripts, summaries, and audio regardless of the retention window chosen. Every deletion and access event is logged to an exportable, SEC Rule 17a-4 and FINRA exam-ready audit trail via the Compliance Portal and Super Admin API.
