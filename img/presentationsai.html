OVERVIEW

What is Presentations.AI?

Presentations.AI is an AI presentation platform that turns ideas, documents, and URLs into professional slide decks in minutes. It automatically applies your brand, helps create compelling content, and exports directly to PowerPoint, making it effortless to go from concept to presentation-ready deck.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Presentations.AI?

Presentations.AI is an AI tool that turns your ideas, documents, or data into ready-to-present slide decks. Give it a prompt, a Word doc, a PDF, or a URL - it builds the narrative, writes the copy, picks the right layout, and applies a clean design. You get a finished presentation in under a minute.

How do I create a presentation with AI?

Start with any input - type a topic, paste your notes, upload a Word doc or PDF, or share a URL. Presentations.AI reads your content, builds a structured deck with clear flow, and applies a clean design. You can edit the slides and export them to PowerPoint or Google Slides in one click.

Can AI create PowerPoint presentations automatically?

Yes. Presentations.AI creates complete PowerPoint decks automatically from your input - no manual setup. You can download the deck as a native .pptx file that opens in Microsoft PowerPoint or Google Slides with all text, layouts, and fonts intact.

Who is Presentations.AI for?

Presentations.AI is built for anyone who needs to turn ideas into decisions through presentations - founders, sales teams, marketers, product managers, consultants, and trainers. It's used across startups, enterprises, and agencies wherever clear decks move work forward.

How is Presentations.AI different from Gamma, Canva, and Copilot?

Most AI presentation tools focus on speed. Presentations.AI focuses on outcomes - clearer decks, faster alignment, fewer follow-up meetings. Gamma is built for web-first, one-time presentations. Canva is a general design tool, not a presentation platform. Copilot works inside PowerPoint but can't connect to live data or generate decks at scale. Presentations.AI is the only platform that combines deck creation, data-connected refresh, and API-based generation in one product.

Who owns the content I create with Presentations.AI?

You own the presentations you create. Content generated through Presentations.AI belongs to you and your team, and you're free to use, edit, and distribute it as you would any other work product.

Can multiple people work on the same presentation at once?

Yes. Presentations.AI supports real-time collaboration, so team members can edit, comment, and review the same deck together, with changes reflected instantly for everyone in the workspace. 

How much can I customize the design beyond what the AI generates?

The AI generates a full first draft, but every element — layout, colors, fonts, imagery, and branding — is fully editable. You can override any AI decision without starting the deck over. 

One Deck, One Team

With Presentations.AI, your team works from one shared version, so everyone's looking at the same deck, in the same workspace, at the same time.
