Safe, secure and private

Go to Privacy & Security

Your meetings are nobody else’s business. Jamie keeps your data in the EU, never stores audio, and never trains models on it, all under ISO 27001, GDPR and DORA.

  • Data residency

    Storage and processing stay within the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK. Your data never leaves these jurisdictions.

  • Data handling

    Audio deleted after transcription. Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2) and at rest (AES 256). Never used to train models.

  • Certifications & compliance

    ISO 27001 certified and GDPR compliant, aligned with the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) for regulated industries.

  • Enterprise controls

    Single sign-on (SAML 2.0), SCIM provisioning, verified domains, and admin controls. Access always stays with your workspace.
