Queries About Our AI PPT Generator? We've Got Answers

Accurate answers to help you succeed with AI-powered presentations

What languages are supported by MagicSlides AI PPT maker?

MagicSlides supports 136+ languages. You can use any language you want.

What do I actually receive after I purchase a license?

You will find an account with the Gmail/Suite ID specified in the purchase, and upgrade it.

Can someone else order the license of AI Presentation Maker on my behalf?

Absolutely. When you make a purchase, the Gmail address specified in the order will be automatically upgraded, and the payer's email is not tied to the account.

How is support provided, and what does it include?

You can send an email to support@magicslides.app while your support period is still valid. Support is provided for AI PPT Maker issues with installation, licensing, and usage. It does not include any customisation or new custom features.

What constitutes a user in terms of pricing?

A user is defined as one Google Account email address (username@domain). Our add-ons are licensed per user (Google Account), and you can use the license on any computer as long as you are signed in with the same email address.

Does MagicSlides AI presentation maker work with both Google Slides and PowerPoint?

Yes. MagicSlides creates presentations in Google Slides by default, and you can easily download them as a PowerPoint (PPTX) file anytime.

Is my data safe when I use the AI Presentation maker?

Absolutely. Your content is never stored or shared. All processing happens securely, and your files remain private.

Can I customise the slides after the AI generates them?

Yes, 100%. Once the AI creates your slides, you can edit the layout, content, design, etc, just like any normal presentation.

Can I use AI PPT maker to update or improve an existing presentation?

Yes. You can upload your existing content or paste your content, and MagicSlides will rebuild the presentation as desired.

Can't find an answer to your question related to AI Presentation Maker?

Send an email to support@magicslides.app, and we'll be happy to help you.
