Plus AI is built for professional slide makers who need to make slides inside of PowerPoint and Google Slides.

Unlike other AI presentation tools, we create native PPTX files (we build and use our own Open XML renderer) and Google Slides presentations that are fully compatible with your existing slide templates and workflows.

Our team is composed of former management consultants, designers, and AI engineers who focus on building the right user interface and user experience that helps our customers get the most out of AI while understanding the limitations (of which there are still many!).

For our enterprise customers, we offer enterprise-grade security and compliance, and we support fully customized presentation templates, so you can create and edit slides that follow your organization's branding and style guidelines.
