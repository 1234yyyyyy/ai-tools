Questions? We have the answers

What makes WellSaid’s AI voice generator the most realistic on the market today?

It comes down to how the voices were built. Most AI voice generator tools train on massive datasets scraped from the internet, which means the voices inherit all the inconsistencies, background noise, and unnatural cadence of that source material. WellSaid takes a different approach. Every voice in our library starts with licensed studio recordings from real voice actors, which gives our AI something much better to learn from. The result is text to speech output that sounds like a person actually said it instead of a computer trying to sound like one.

How is WellSaid different from other text to speech tools?

Most tools are optimized for volume — lots of voices, fast output, and cheap pricing. WellSaid's AI voice generator is optimized for quality and for teams that need to maintain that quality across a lot of content. That means 240+ voice models with real regional accents, a collaborative studio where your whole team can work on the same projects, and a developer-ready text-to-speech API for teams that need to build AI-speech applications into their products. It also means a closed-model AI that keeps your scripted audio and voice samples private, something our competitors can't say.

What languages does WellSaid’s AI voice generator support?

WellSaid supports 18 languages across regional variants, including Arabic, Chinese (Cantonese and Mandarin), Czech, Danish, Dutch (Belgium and Netherlands), English (multiple regional variants), French (Belgium, Canada, and France), German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Persian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Spanish (Spain and Mexico), Swedish, and Turkish, with more on the way.

What matters as much as the list is the quality. Our multilingual AI voices are built on regional recordings, so the text-to-speech output sounds like a local speaker. That distinction matters a lot when you're producing training content or ads for a specific market.

Is WellSaid secure enough for enterprise use?

Yes, and it was built that way rather than tacked on later. WellSaid is SOC 2 and GDPR compliant, and uses a closed-model AI architecture so your voice over scripts and AI voice samples never leave your environment or train external models. Every audio file from our text-to-speech generator comes with full commercial usage rights, and there's clear documentation ready for legal, brand, and procurement reviews.

Can I use WellSaid’s AI voices for commercial use cases?

Yes, across the board. Every AI voice in the library includes full commercial usage rights. That covers paid ads, audio advertising, product marketing, brand campaigns, elearning content, and anything else you're making with our text to speech generator. Solo creators and enterprise teams are covered the same way.

Does WellSaid have a text to speech API?

Yes. The WellSaid text-to-speech API is built for engineering teams that need to bring studio-quality text to speech output directly into their own products and workflows. It uses the same voices available in the studio, so what your developers ship sounds as good as what your creative team produces. Full documentation, authentication details, and API references are available in the developer docs.

Can multiple team members collaborate in WellSaid?
