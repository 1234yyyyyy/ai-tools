How Read AI Works

From the invite to the follow-through

From meeting recaps to Ask Read, uncover the insights you need—when you need them.

Walk in
ready

  • Your team's context from calendar, email, past meetings, and CRM
  • Know what was promised last time and what's still open
  • Delivered to your inbox, tailored to you

Capture what was said

  • Works across any platform including Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams
  • Auto-generated recaps, action items, and highlights custom to you
  • Everyone in the meeting knows Read is there — enterprise-grade privacy by default

Meeting ends, actions start

  • Action items routed to the tools your team already works in
  • Ask what was decided, who owns it, and what's still open — with citations
  • Everything shared becomes context your whole team can search
