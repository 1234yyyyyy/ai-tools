Questions

What counts as an external user?

Each distinct user of your app who connects at least one account through Connect, identified by the external user ID you pass. One user with five connected apps counts once.

What happens when I pass 100 external users?

Nothing breaks — each additional user is $2 per month on Startup. Scaling past a few thousand users? Talk to us about volume pricing.

What are usage credits?

Credits measure Connect API usage. Running an action, calling a tool through MCP, sending a request through the Connect proxy, and emitting an event from a deployed trigger each use 1 credit per 30 seconds of compute. Listing apps and accounts, configuring components, and other management calls don't use credits. Startup includes 10,000 credits each month; after that, each credit is $0.012, and the rate drops at higher volume.

What's the difference between development and production?

Development gives you full API, SDK, and MCP access to build and test, free. Serving real external users in production requires Startup.

How do I cancel or downgrade?

Self-serve, any time, from your workspace's billing settings. Before you confirm, we show whether the change applies now or at the end of your billing period.
